About

launch pads

Motivated and goal-oriented, I bring five years of legal office experience, a consultative sales background ranked in the top 3 at Sunglass Hut, and a deep passion for entrepreneurship and wellness to everything I build.

I’m completing my B.A. in Integrated Studies with a Minor in Entrepreneurship at Florida Gulf Coast University. As President of the CEO Club and a founder in FGCU’s Runway Incubator, I’m building Lowkief — a cannabis wellness accessory brand — while creating content that empowers aspiring entrepreneurs. I believe marijuana has a taboo string attached that is long overdue to be cut off.
