About

From legal offices to
launch pads

Motivated and goal-oriented, I bring five years of legal office experience, a consultative sales background ranked top-3 across multiple Sunglass Hut locations, and a deep passion for entrepreneurship and wellness to everything I build.

I'm currently completing my B.A. in Integrated Studies with a Minor in Entrepreneurship at Florida Gulf Coast University. As President of the CEO Club and a founder in FGCU's Runway Incubator Program, I'm developing Lowkief — a cannabis wellness accessory startup — while creating content that empowers aspiring entrepreneurs to take their first steps. When I'm not building, you can find me exploring new places, making art, or diving into political science.
